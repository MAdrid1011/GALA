# NCU launch-signature plan

`python -m gala_sim.tools.gpu_ncu_plan --config <yaml> --nsys-profile <json> ... --stability-nsys-profile <json> ... --output <json>` validates two complete sets of frozen representative NSYS inventories and creates a measured-counter plan. Non-range launch order must match exactly across the two sets; configured range stages may change multiplicity, but their signature sets must match. The formal runner rejects a plan without passed repeat stability. After NCU runs, `python -m gala_sim.tools.gpu_ncu_plan --plan <plan.json> --ncu-profile <json> ... --output <evidence.json>` validates measured CSV-derived profiles against every required launch and signature. Use `gpu_ncu_runner preflight` for the short, first-window NCU overhead/utilization gate; it deliberately uses common metrics rather than full-run invocation ordinals.

The signature retains stage, demangled kernel name, grid, and block. Multiplicity remains separate for every representative iteration. First, middle, and last observed occurrences are selected to test counter reuse; a signature may be multiplied by its exact NSYS multiplicity only when not every occurrence is measured and the sampled counters agree. When every occurrence is measured, differing counters are retained and summed per launch instead of being reused.

NCU runs with `--profile-from-start off` while `stage_runner` opens CUDA Profiler API windows at all frozen representative iterations. Stable stages use invocation jobs. Stages listed in `range_capture_stages` are excluded before invocation numbering and are captured in a separate job through exact process-wide `gala_ncu_stage` start/end range expressions. The range job profiles every launch in those stages and reselects first, middle, and last anchors from the observed exact signature; multiplicity may change, but a missing or new signature remains a failure. This split is backed by a same-process NCU probe showing that NVTX filtering precedes invocation numbering.

The plan groups the remaining kernel names and invocation ordinals into the configured maximum number of complete-training jobs. Group merging may measure additional deterministic launches, and invocation jobs still bind every such launch to its NSYS identity. A range job binds its required signature anchors and retains all other observed launches for counter agreement and SASS classification. `gpu_profile_artifacts ncu-job` also binds the NCU process ID, stage-profile process ID, campaign hash, plan hash, job index, stage, name, and shape. The generated arguments do not use `--launch-count` or `--kill`, and every official process must naturally execute the complete training path.

The plan is not performance evidence. It remains ineligible until counters, dynamic SASS semantics, local calibration, and AGX Orin calibration pass their gates.
